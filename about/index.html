About Rana Aryan

Student • Builder • Creator

Rana Aryan is a Class 11 PCM student from India, web software developer, and indie builder. Documenting his work publicly at ranaaryandev.github.io, Rana shares his projects, software experiments, learning journey, and web development ideas. Driven by curiosity and software craft, his work includes local-first tools and web applications like Lumina Engine, Tactile Specs, Pulse Privacy, and Papyrus CLI. Rana's goal is to learn by building, making mistakes, and sharing transparent notes on web development and student life across GitHub, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, and Substack.

Background & Philosophy

Hi, I’m Rana.

I’m a Class 11 PCM student from India who likes building things on the web. I don’t have some perfect story about how I got into tech. Honestly, I just enjoy making websites. An idea comes into my head, I start building it, something breaks, I fix it, and somehow I end up learning something new.

This website is basically a record of that process.

Here, I’m putting my projects, experiments, ideas, things I’m learning, and sometimes probably things that make absolutely no sense outside my head. I want this to be more than just a portfolio. Think of it as my little corner of the internet where I can keep track of what I’m building and how I’m growing.

I’m still in Class 11, so I’m nowhere near where I want to be. There’s a lot I don’t know yet, a lot I need to improve, and honestly, when I finish something, I can usually point out at least ten things I could have done better.

But that’s kind of the point.

I’m trying to use these years to build skills, try things, make mistakes, and see how far I can take myself before college even starts. I want to enter college having already built meaningful skills, useful things, and a body of work I'm proud to show.

Not just because of coding, but because of everything — the problem-solving mindset, technical craft, independence, and the tools built along the way.

Building every week, learning in public, and improving step by step.

If you want to see what I’m working on, what I’m learning, or where all of this goes, you’re welcome to stick around.
